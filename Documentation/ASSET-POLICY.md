# Asset and Intellectual Property Policy

CAR DRIVE PARKING is intended to be an original game.

## Vehicle designs and brands
- Use original fictional vehicle manufacturers, names, badges and designs.
- Do not copy real manufacturer logos or trademarked badges.
- Do not reproduce a real vehicle design so closely that it creates avoidable intellectual-property risk.
- Treat all proposed brand and model names as provisional until trademark and domain checks are completed.

## 3D models, textures, audio and fonts
- Prefer original assets or assets with a license that clearly permits the intended commercial use.
- Record the source, author, license and any attribution requirements for every third-party asset.
- Do not assume that an asset is free to use commercially just because it can be downloaded for free.
- Keep license files and attribution notices in the repository where practical.

## Maps and real-world references
- Neustadt is a fictional city.
- Use original road layouts, signage and environmental compositions.
- Do not copy commercial game maps or proprietary assets.

## AI-generated assets
- Review generated assets for accidental logos, protected characters, recognizable brand marks or copied designs.
- Keep a record of the generation source and any relevant usage terms.

## Before release
- Review trademarks, asset licenses, privacy requirements and platform requirements.
- Obtain qualified legal advice for unresolved rights questions.
